How it listens

The analyzer works in three passes.

Search for a song, paste a YouTube link, or upload a file. SoundMind trims silence, extracts acoustic features, optionally measures isolated vocals, and then maps the result into a readable interpretation.

I.

Core acoustic pass

Measures tempo, key and mode, energy, loudness, acousticness, danceability, speechiness, and spectral texture after trimming leading and trailing silence.

II.

Optional vocal pass

If Demucs and Praat are available, the analyzer separates the vocal stem and measures F0 mean, range, variation, shimmer, and vocal harmonic clarity.

III.

Section and confidence pass

Builds a section arc, scores estimated valence and arousal over time, and reports ambiguity when tempo or key detection looks uncertain.

Tier 1 — tempo, key, loudness, texture
Tier 2/3 — vocals, section arc, warnings
