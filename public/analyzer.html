Listen beneath the surface

Every song leaves a physiological fingerprint.

SoundMind examines the measurable structure behind a track, then frames those signals through established models of emotion. The result is not a genre label. It is a closer look at how the music moves.

Measure the signal

Tempo, pulse stability, loudness, spectral balance, dynamics, and tonal center establish the track's acoustic profile.

Isolate the voice

Optional stem separation examines vocal pitch, range, shimmer, and harmonic clarity away from the full mix.

Map the feeling

Valence and arousal place the track on an interactive emotional map, with a plain-language interpretation.
